The whole point

A wrong number you can't trace is worse than no number at all.

Every AI tool that reads documents makes mistakes — misreads a smudged total, confuses two similar dates, invents a value that isn't there. The difference isn't whether errors happen. It's whether you can catch them.

Generic extractors and chat tools give you an answer floating free of its source. To check it, you re-open the document and hunt. Most people don't — they trust it, and the error slips through into an invoice, a contract summary, or a claim.

Groundtruth is built on Google's open-source LangExtract, which anchors every extracted value to its exact span in the original text. We surface that as an interactive highlight. You get the speed of automation with the confidence of having checked it yourself — because checking takes one click.

Trustworthy data isn't data that's always right. It's data you can always verify.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How accurate is the extraction?

Accuracy depends on document quality — but that's not really the point, because no automated tool is perfect. What makes Groundtruth different is that you don't have to take our word for it. Every field is highlighted in the source, so you can verify each value in one click and correct anything that's off before you export.

What happens to my documents? Is it private?

Your documents are processed to produce your results and are not used to train any models. You can delete uploads and results at any time. Paid plans are covered by a Data Processing Agreement. For sensitive workflows we recommend Pro and Business, which give tighter control over retention and access.

How is this different from just using ChatGPT?

ChatGPT can extract fields, but it hands you an answer with no way to check where it came from — and will occasionally invent values that sound right. Groundtruth grounds every field to the exact location in your document and highlights it, gives you repeatable templates and structured exports (JSON/CSV/API), and is built for batches and workflows, not one-off questions.

What file types do you support?

PDFs, plain text files and pasted text today — covering the vast majority of invoices, contracts, reports, CVs and notes people process by hand. Scanned/image-only PDFs and more formats are on the roadmap.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. All paid plans are month-to-month, no contract, no cancellation fee. Cancel from your dashboard and keep access until the end of your billing period. The Free plan is free forever, no card required.

Can I extract custom fields, not just templates?

Absolutely. On any plan you can describe the fields you want in plain English. On Pro and Business you can save reusable custom schemas so your team extracts the same fields the same way every time.

Do you have an API?

Yes — included on Pro and Business. Send documents and get grounded, structured data back programmatically. Business plans add webhooks so results push to your systems automatically.

How secure is the platform?

We use encryption in transit and at rest, scoped access controls and a strict no-training policy on your data. Business plans support team roles and access management. For specific compliance requirements, contact us and we'll walk you through our controls.
