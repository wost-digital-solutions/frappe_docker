### How It Works (3 steps)

1. **Upload or paste.** Drop in a PDF, a text file, or paste raw text. One document or a whole batch.
2. **Pick a template or describe your fields.** Choose a ready-made schema, or type what you need: "vendor name, invoice number, line items, total, due date." No syntax to learn.
3. **Get grounded data back.** Review your results in an interactive highlighted viewer, fix anything that looks off, and export to JSON, CSV, or a table — or push it to your systems via API.

### Why Grounding Matters (Trust Section)

**A wrong number you can't trace is worse than no number at all.**

Every AI tool that reads documents makes mistakes — misreads a smudged total, confuses two similar dates, invents a value that isn't there. The difference isn't whether errors happen. It's whether you can catch them.

Generic extractors and chat-based tools give you an answer floating free of its source. To check it, you re-open the document and hunt. Most people don't — they trust it, and the error slips through into an invoice, a contract summary, or a claim.

Groundtruth is built on Google's open-source **LangExtract** library, which anchors every extracted value to its exact span in the original text. We surface that grounding as an interactive highlight. You get the speed of automated extraction with the confidence of having checked it yourself — because checking takes one click.

*Trustworthy structured data isn't data that's always right. It's data you can always verify.*

### FAQ

**How accurate is the extraction?**
Accuracy depends on document quality, but that's not really the point — no automated tool is perfect. What makes Groundtruth different is that you don't have to take our word for it. Every field is highlighted in the source document, so you can verify each value in one click and correct anything that's off before you export. You get speed and a built-in way to catch mistakes.

**What happens to my documents and data? Is it private?**
Your documents are processed to produce your results and are not used to train any models. You can delete uploads and results at any time. Paid plans are covered by a Data Processing Agreement. For sensitive workflows, we recommend the Pro and Business plans, which give you tighter control over retention and access. *(See our Privacy Policy and DPA for full details.)*

**How is this different from just using ChatGPT?**
ChatGPT can extract fields, but it hands you an answer with no way to check where it came from — and it will occasionally invent values that sound right. Groundtruth grounds every field in the exact location in your document and highlights it, gives you repeatable templates and structured exports (JSON/CSV/API) instead of copy-pasting from a chat, and is built for batches and workflows, not one-off questions.

**What file types do you support?**
PDFs, plain text files, and pasted text today. That covers the vast majority of invoices, contracts, reports, CVs, and notes people process by hand. Scanned/image-only PDFs and additional formats are on our roadmap.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. All paid plans are month-to-month with no contract and no cancellation fee. Cancel from your dashboard and you keep access until the end of your billing period. The Free plan is free forever, no card required.

**How secure is the platform?**
We use encryption in transit and at rest, scoped access controls, and a strict no-training policy on your data. Business plans support team roles and access management. If you have specific security or compliance requirements, contact us and we'll walk you through our controls.

**Can I extract custom fields, not just the templates?**
Absolutely. On any plan you can describe the fields you want in plain English. On Pro and Business you can save reusable **custom schemas**, so your team extracts the same structured fields the same way every time — no re-describing, no drift.

**Do you have an API?**
Yes. The API is included on Pro and Business. Send documents and get grounded, structured data back programmatically — ideal for wiring Groundtruth into your existing tools. Business plans add **webhooks** so results can be pushed to your systems automatically.
